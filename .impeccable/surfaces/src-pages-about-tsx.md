## Direction contract

THESIS: The About page is the back of the pouch: one gold-ruled information panel families already know how to read. It refuses the hero image plus vision/mission cards template.

OWN-WORLD: Three inks only: emerald-950 ground, gold #C9A45C label cells with dark uppercase labels, cream values. Fraunces light for values, Figtree for labels. 2px gold panel frame, 1px gold rules between rows, 14px radius. No photos, no ornament.

STORY: The visitor reads Product, then all 21 Ingredients, then Our Vision and Our Mission, then Manufactured by (with address), then Certified (FSSAI, ISO), and acts: Shop Now, or Get in Touch.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left column ~360px: h1 "Know About Us More" (gold italic "Us More") and the gold Shop Now button. Right: the label panel, rows Product / Ingredients / Our Vision / Our Mission / Manufactured by / Certified. Mobile: heading, then panel full width with label cells stacked above values, Shop Now after the heading.

Signature interaction: the panel prints like a label being stamped. Each gold label cell fills left to right in sequence and its value fades up behind it, once on load. Ingredient names highlight on hover. Motion grammar: one ease-out-quart entrance, ~0.6s per row, reduced motion shows the finished label.

Adaptations (from the finish review): value cells sit on emerald-900, one step up from the ground, and secondary text uses mist (emerald-tinted cream) and gold-300 tints. These stay inside the emerald/gold/cream ink family and exist for contrast hierarchy between primary and secondary values.

Copy: the lead line "Read us the way you read the back of our pack." was approved by the user. The "Goodness" row label is new wording taken from the pack's "With the GOODNESS of" line; its claims are the flyer's own wording (spelling of cholesterol / trans fat corrected); still to be confirmed with the client.
